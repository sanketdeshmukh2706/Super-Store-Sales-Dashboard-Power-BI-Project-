# 🏪 Super Store Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Sales Dashboard** developed using Power BI to analyze Super Store data. It provides meaningful insights into sales performance, profit trends, customer segments, and regional distribution.

---

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify top-performing categories and regions
* Track monthly sales and profit trends
* Enable data-driven business decision-making

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Excel / CSV Dataset
* Power Query (Data Cleaning & Transformation)
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

---

## 📁 Dataset

* Super Store Sales Dataset
* Includes: Orders, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Category, Region, Ship Mode

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* KPI Cards (Sales, Profit, Quantity, Average Delivery Time)
* Sales by Category and Sub-Category
* Region-wise and State-wise Analysis
* Monthly Sales & Profit Trends (Year-over-Year)
* Sales by Customer Segment and Payment Mode
* Shipping Mode Analysis
* Interactive Filters (Region Selection)

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 🔹 Sales Performance

* Total sales reached **1.57M**
* The West region contributes the highest share of sales

---

### 🔹 Category Analysis

* Office Supplies is the top-performing category
* Technology and Furniture also contribute significantly

---

### 🔹 Sub-Category Analysis

* Phones generate the highest revenue
* Chairs and Binders follow

---

### 🔹 Regional Insights

* West and East regions dominate overall sales
* South region has the lowest contribution

---

### 🔹 Shipping Insights

* Standard Class is the most preferred shipping mode
* Same Day delivery is the least used

---

### 🔹 Payment Insights

* Cash on Delivery (COD) is the most used payment method
* Online payments also show strong adoption

---

### 🔹 Monthly Trends

* Sales and profit increased in 2020 compared to 2019
* Peak performance observed in November and December
